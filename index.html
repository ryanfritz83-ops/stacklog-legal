A look inside

Built to take a few seconds a day.

Open it, tap what you took, close it. Consistency, supply estimates and reminders take care of themselves.

Features

Everything a daily stack needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

1

Today, grouped by timing

Your list is split into AM, with meals and PM, with one-tap Taken or Skip for every dose.

2

Your custom daily log

Set the dose, timing and days of the week for each supplement or vitamin.

3

12-week consistency grid

See at a glance how consistently you’ve stuck to your scheduled stack.

4

Supply & cost estimates

Days-left estimates for each bottle, plus an estimated monthly cost for your stack.

5

Local reminders

Optional dose and refill reminders — mark a dose Taken right from the notification banner.

6

Backup & clinician summary

Export a backup JSON file, or a summary you can share with your clinician.

Privacy

Your stack is nobody’s business but yours.

StackLog Daily is local-only. There’s nothing to sign up for and nowhere for your data to go.

  • No account to create — open the app and start logging.
  • No cloud sync. Your log is stored on your iPhone.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • You choose when to export a backup or a clinician summary.
  • No analytics, advertising SDKs or crash reporters in the app.
  • No tracking across apps or websites. This page has no cookies or trackers either.

StackLog Daily is a tracker. It isn’t medical advice and isn’t intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to a clinician before changing your supplements. Read the full privacy policy.
